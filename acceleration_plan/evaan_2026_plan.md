# Evaan's 9-Month Academic Acceleration Plan (2026)
## March - December 2026 | 1st Grade → 3rd Grade Level

---

## Executive Summary

**Student:** Evaan (Age 7)  
**Current Level:** Above-average 1st grader  
**Goal:** Reach 3rd grade proficiency by December 2026  
**Timeline:** 9 months (March - December 2026)  
**Daily Time Commitment:** 60-90 minutes (structured) + reading time

This plan accelerates through 2 years of curriculum (2nd + 3rd grade) in 9 months through focused daily work, strategic subject rotation, and integrated learning experiences.

## Weekly Rotation Schedule

To maintain engagement and prevent burnout, subjects rotate on a 4-week cycle with built-in variety:

### Week Types

| Week | Primary Focus (40%) | Secondary Focus (30%) | Rotation Subjects (20%) | Enrichment (10%) |
|------|--------------------|----------------------|------------------------|------------------|
| **Week 1** | Math | ELA | Science | Art/Movement |
| **Week 2** | ELA | Math | Social Studies | Music/Culture |
| **Week 3** | Science + Math | ELA | Social Studies | Hands-on Project |
| **Week 4** | Social Studies + ELA | Math | Science | Creative Writing |

### Daily Structure (60-90 min)

| Time | Activity | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 15-20 min | Core Subject | Primary focus of the week |
| 15-20 min | Secondary Subject | Supporting skill building |
| 10-15 min | Rotation Subject | Shorter, engaging topic |
| 10-15 min | Reading | Independent or read-aloud |
| 5-10 min | Review/Game | Reinforcement activity |

## Parent/Guardian Notes

### Weekly Check-ins
- Review work at end of each week
- Discuss challenges and victories
- Adjust pace if needed (faster or slower)
- Maintain positive attitude about learning

### Red Flags to Watch
- Frustration or resistance
- Consistent errors in same concept
- Decreased interest or engagement
- Physical signs of stress

### Adjustments
This plan is a guide, not a rigid schedule. If Evaan excels in one area, accelerate. If he needs more time, slow down. The goal is mastery, not speed.

### Communication
- Monthly progress reviews
- Celebrate milestones
- Document growth with work samples
- Adjust goals as needed

## Final Notes

This acceleration plan is ambitious but achievable for an above-average 7-year-old with consistent support. The key is maintaining engagement through variety, celebrating progress, and adjusting as needed. Remember that "above grade level" also means different things in different subjects—Evaan may soar ahead in math while needing more support in writing, or vice versa. Follow his lead and interests while ensuring balanced development across all subjects.

**Most important:** Learning should be joyful. If Evaan is stressed, tired, or resistant, slow down. If he's excited and engaged, speed up. The goal is a love of learning that will serve him throughout his academic career and beyond.
