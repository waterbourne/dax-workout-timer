### OPPORTUNITY #1: Herman Miller & High-End Office Chairs
**Category:** Furniture / Office Equipment  
**Effort Level:** Low-Medium | **Risk:** Low | **Priority: HIGHEST**

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Buy Price (Local)** | $350-$550 (Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, estate sales) |
| **Sell Price (eBay)** | $600-$900 (Aeron Size B), $750-$1,200 (fully loaded) |
| **Profit Margin** | 40-60% after fees/shipping |
| **Time to Sell** | 1-2 weeks |

**Why It Works:**
- Remote work normalized high-end home office setups
- Herman Miller Aerons retail for $1,800+ new
- Bay Area tech workers constantly moving/selling

**Authentication/Condition Check:**
- Size marked on back of chair (A=small, B=medium, C=large)
- Check for fully adjustable arms (high-value feature)
- Verify lumbar support pad is present
- Test all tilt functions
- Look for manufacturing date sticker (under seat)

**Risks:**
- Heavy to ship (~50 lbs) - offer local pickup or charge $100+ shipping
- Counterfeits exist but rare - buy from verified sellers only
- Missing parts reduce value significantly

**Tonight's Action:**
1. Search Facebook Marketplace "Herman Miller" within 25 miles
2. Set up saved search alerts for "Aeron" and "Steelcase Leap"
3. Check OfferUp and Craigslist simultaneously
4. Message 3-5 sellers with cash-ready offers

### OPPORTUNITY #2: iPhones & Flagship Smartphones
**Category:** Electronics  
**Effort Level:** Medium | **Risk:** Medium | **Priority: HIGH**

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Buy Price (Local)** | $300-$600 (iPhone 13-14 Pro, depending on storage/condition) |
| **Sell Price (Swappa/eBay)** | $450-$800 |
| **Profit Margin** | 20-35% after fees |
| **Time to Sell** | 3-7 days |

**Why It Works:**
- Constant upgrade cycle creates supply
- iPhones hold value better than Android
- Carrier-locked phones sell for less (opportunity to unlock)

**Authentication/Condition Check:**
- Check IMEI on Swappa/IMEI.info (must be clean, not blacklisted)
- Verify iCloud is removed ( Settings > General > Transfer or Reset)
- Test all cameras, Face ID, charging port
- Check battery health (Settings > Battery > Battery Health)
- Water damage indicators in SIM tray

**Risks:**
- High scam potential on eBay - use Swappa for safer sales
- IMEI blacklisting after purchase (buy from verified owners only)
- Battery replacement costs $50-80 if needed

**Tonight's Action:**
1. Search Facebook Marketplace "iPhone 14 Pro" max price $550
2. Filter by "Sellers" not "Dealers" to avoid stolen goods
3. Prepare IMEI verification process before meeting

## RISK MITIGATION CHECKLIST

**Before Any Purchase:**
- [ ] Check sold comps on eBay (last 30 days)
- [ ] Verify seller has positive history
- [ ] Meet in public place for local deals
- [ ] Test all functions before buying
- [ ] Factor in all costs (fees, shipping, materials, time)

**Red Flags - Walk Away:**
- Seller won't meet in person for high-value items
- Price is 50%+ below market with no explanation
- Serial numbers removed or defaced
- Seller rushes you or pushes for unusual payment methods
- "Too many" brand new tools from one seller
