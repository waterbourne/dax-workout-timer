- "Focus on form over speed — quality reps only."
- "Beat yesterday's round count if possible."

### 📋 THE CIRCUIT
Format: "□ Exercise Name: sets × reps (modification)"
- □ Push-ups: 3×12
- □ Goblet Squats: 3×15 (use backpack)
- □ Plank Hold: 3×45s

### ⏱️ TIMING
- "12 minutes total | 60 sec rest between rounds"
- "15 minutes | Rest as needed"

- Why this combination works
- The benefit/physiology

