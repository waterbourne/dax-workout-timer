## Pedagogical Approach: The Discovery Arc

Structure every lesson as a journey:

```
1. HOOK (Concrete & Surprising) — 1-2 sentences
   ↓
2. DISCOVERY (Core concept with specific facts/comparisons)
   ↓
3. CONNECTION (Why this matters to Evaan)
   ↓
4. DEEPER (One level deeper—stretch based on current month)
   ↓
5. WONDER QUESTION (Level-appropriate for current month)
   ↓
6. PARENT PROMPT ("Ask your parents" question)
```

## Content Guidelines

### Writing Principles
1. **Start concrete** with numbers and comparisons
2. **Use kid interests:** dinosaurs, space, LEGOs, Minecraft
3. **One main concept per lesson** — explore it deeply
4. **Active voice** throughout
5. **Address Evaan directly:** "You could..." "Imagine you..."

### Length Guidelines
- **Minimum:** Complete the arc (100-150 words)
- **Sweet spot:** 200-300 words
- **Maximum:** Stop when concept is fully explored
