- "Look around your house. Bridges, bike frames, and shelves all use triangles. Your front door probably has a diagonal brace — that's a triangle making it strong!"

Specific question for parents:
- "Ask your parents: What other shapes do engineers use to make things strong?"

### Example 2: Space/Scale
```
📚 YOU COULD FIT 1 MILLION EARTHS INSIDE THE SUN!

🤯 THE BIG IDEA
The Sun is so gigantic that a million Earths could fit inside it — but it looks tiny in the sky!

🔬 HOW DISTANCE WORKS
• Things look smaller when they're far away
• The Sun is 93 million miles from Earth
• If Earth was a marble, the Sun would be a yoga ball 800 feet away

💡 REAL WORLD
Hold a penny at arm's length — it can cover the Moon! That's how the tiny-looking Sun can light up our whole planet.

🧪 TRY THIS
Get a ball (Sun) and a pea (Earth). Place them 26 feet apart. That's the real scale! Walk between them to feel the distance.

❓ WONDER QUESTION
Ask your parents: How long would it take to drive to the Sun at highway speed?
```
**iOD: 8 | Words: 128**

### Example 3: Dinosaurs
```
📚 T-REX COULD CRUSH A CAR WITH ONE BITE!

🤯 THE BIG IDEA
T-Rex had a bite force of 12,800 pounds — strong enough to crush a car or bite through solid bone!

🔬 HOW T-REX HUNTED
• 60 saw-edged teeth like steak knives
• Binocular vision to judge distance
• Could run 12-17 mph (as fast as a pro cyclist!)

💡 REAL WORLD
Stand next to a parked car. T-Rex was that tall (12 feet at the hips) and could bite through the roof! But you could outrun it on a bike.

🧪 TRY THIS
Measure 40 feet (T-Rex length) with a tape measure. That's as long as a school bus! Walk the length to feel how huge they were.

❓ WONDER QUESTION
Ask your parents: What modern animal has the strongest bite today?
```
