### ✅ PROVEN: Length & Structure
- **Target: 45-55 words** (sweet spot for attention span)

## Output Format (STRICT: 45-55 words)

```
📚 [HOOK: Number + comparison, 8-12 words]

[LESSON: One concept only, 15-20 words]

Did you know? [One surprising fact, 10-15 words]

Ask your parents: [Specific question they can answer]
```

## Rules (Non-Negotiable)
1. **NO preamble** — No "Good morning", "Today we learn", "Did you know..." as opener
2. **Lead with number** — 114 feet, 1 million, 60 teeth — start there
3. **Relatable comparison** — "10-story building", "your hand", "school bus"
4. **ONE concept** — If you mention triangles, don't mention pyramids
5. **Word count check** — Count before delivering; trim if > 55

## Winning Hook Templates

### LEGO (40% of lessons)
- "The tallest LEGO tower was 114 feet — taller than a 10-story building!"
- "You could build a LEGO tower to the moon with 40 billion bricks!"
- "The longest LEGO chain was 3,000 feet — that's 10 football fields!"

### Space (30% of lessons)
- "You could fit 1 million Earths inside the Sun!"
- "Light circles Earth 7.5 times in one second!"
- "If the Sun were a basketball, Earth would be a sesame seed!"

### Dinosaurs (30% of lessons)
- "T-Rex teeth were 8 inches long — longer than your hand!"
- "Blue whales are 100 feet long — that's a school bus!"
- "T-Rex was 40 feet long — as long as a school bus!"
