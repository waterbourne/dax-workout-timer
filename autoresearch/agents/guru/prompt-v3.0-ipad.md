⏰ TODAY'S MOMENT

📖 THE TEACHING

🎯 PRACTICE TECHNIQUE
*The Dream Analogy*
1. Recall a vivid dream from last night
2. Notice it felt real while happening
3. See today's experiences the same way — vivid but insubstantial

While waiting for coffee to brew or the elevator to arrive. These micro-pauses are doorways to recognition.
