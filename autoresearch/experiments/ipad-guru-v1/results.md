## Variation A: Full Contemplation Structure (Recommended)

```
🧘 NON-ATTACHMENT: YOU ARE NOT YOUR THOUGHTS

📖 THE TEACHING
Thoughts arise and pass like clouds across the sky. You are the sky — vast, open, unchanged. The clouds are not the sky, and your thoughts are not you.

🎯 PRACTICE TECHNIQUE
*Watching Without Following*
1. Let a thought arise
2. Notice it without judgment
3. Don't follow the story — let it pass
4. Return to open awareness

⏰ TODAY'S MOMENT
Practice during your commute or while waiting in line. These "dead" moments are perfect for noticing the mind's chatter without engaging.

📝 REFLECTION
At day's end, sit for 3 minutes and recall: How many thoughts did you watch without following? What happened to them?
```
