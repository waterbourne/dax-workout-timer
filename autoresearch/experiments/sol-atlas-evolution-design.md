# Sol & Atlas Experimental Design
## Pedagogical Evolution Framework for 7-Year-Old (Grade 2 → 3)

### Core Hypothesis
Content depth matters more than brevity for educational engagement. The key is **scaffolded complexity** that grows with Evaan over the year.

### Evolution Stages (12-Month Progression)

| Month | Cognitive Stage | Sol Approach | Atlas Approach |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1-3 | Concrete (now) | Single facts, tangible comparisons | Simple stories, clear morals |
| 4-6 | Connecting | Cause-effect, "what if" questions | Multiple perspectives, empathy |
| 7-9 | Abstracting | Patterns, systems thinking | Complex characters, nuance |
| 10-12 | Synthesizing | Cross-subject connections | Historical context, philosophy |

### Variables to Test

#### 1. Story Arc vs Fact-Delivery
- **Hypothesis**: Atlas (philosophy) should use narrative arcs; Sol (academic) should use discovery arcs
- **Test**: Run 3 days story-based vs 3 days fact-based
- **Metric**: Follow-up questions asked (higher = better)

#### 2. Concrete vs Abstract Openings
- **Hypothesis**: Start concrete, end with "stretch" question
- **Test**: Compare "dinosaurs" openers to "gravity" openers
- **Metric**: Engagement within 30 min of delivery

#### 3. Question Depth Progression
- **Hypothesis**: Ending questions should get harder over time
- **Test**: Level 1 (yes/no) → Level 2 (what/who) → Level 3 (why/how) → Level 4 (what if)
- **Metric**: Parent reports on discussion quality

### No Word Limit Rule
- Sol: 100-300 words (allows for mini-lessons)
- Atlas: 200-500 words (allows for stories)
- Focus on **completeness of concept** not brevity

### Success Metrics
1. **Sol**: Evaan brings up lesson later that day (measured by parent feedback)
2. **Atlas**: Evaan asks follow-up "why" or "what if" questions
3. **Both**: Content appropriate for grade 2 now, grade 3 by year's end

### Test Schedule

- Week 2: Story-arc format
- Week 3: Discovery-arc format
- Week 4: Hybrid winner
