## Variation A: Full iPad Structure (Recommended)

```
📚 THE EIFFEL TOWER'S SECRET: 1,665 TRIANGLES!

🤯 THE BIG IDEA
The Eiffel Tower stays standing because it's built from triangles — the world's strongest shape!

🔬 HOW TRIANGLES WORK
• Push a square → it turns into a diamond (weak!)
• Push a triangle → it stays strong (powerful!)
• That's why LEGO towers use triangles too

💡 REAL WORLD
Look around your house. Bridges, bike frames, and shelves all use triangles. Your front door probably has a diagonal brace — that's a triangle making it strong!

🧪 TRY THIS
Build the tallest tower you can with blocks or cups. Now add diagonal pieces to make triangles. Can you make it taller without it falling?

❓ WONDER QUESTION
Ask your parents: What other shapes do engineers use to make things strong?
```
